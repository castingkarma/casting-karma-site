Per-touch model: each touch converts your rate of the contacts not yet converted, so cumulative conversion after k touches = 1−(1−rate)^k. Subs = contacts × cumulative rate; MRR = subs × sub price. Time to goal = (contacts needed × touches) ÷ daily sends. Assumes no churn, steady sending, and ignores drip spacing — high-level planning math, not a forecast. Real reply/sub data anchors it once sending starts.
